Mathematical Privacy & Zero-Retention Architecture

SECTION 02 // TECHNICAL COVENANT

Mainstream health applications operate as veiled surveillance brokers—harvesting biometric photographs, dietary weaknesses, and health anxieties to package and auction to programmatic advertising exchanges and health insurance underwriters. PureScan AI was architected from the bare silicon level on the opposite premise: Absolute Data Sovereignty.

01

Ephemeral In-Memory Computation (RAM-Only Zero Persistence)

When a physical label is scanned or an image frame is ingested, bytes reside strictly within volatile RAM buffers encrypted in memory. The optical character recognition and chemical analysis execute atomically. The moment the classification vector is calculated, the raw image buffer is purged with a cryptographically secure zero-fill. No image file is ever written to remote disk storage.

02

Mathematical Rejection of Biometric & Facial Identifiers

The vision ingest pipeline enforces mathematical bounding constraints dedicated exclusively to typographical bounding boxes, nutritional tables, and ingredient strings. Any incidental capture of human faces, skin tone markers, or biometric contours is actively filtered out before ingestion. PureScan AI possesses zero biometric surveillance capabilities.

03

Total Exclusion of Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

PureScan AI requires neither phone numbers, national IDs, nor biometric login gates. Analysis tokens pass through an anonymous client-side gateway. No device advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID) or longitudinal behavioral telemetry are ever associated with your nutritional audit inquiries.

04

Sovereign Data Portability: Bidirectional CSV Serialization & Nuclear Purge

Your dietary audit history is entirely your property. PureScan implements full client-side bidirectional CSV export and import routines, allowing complete backup and offline analysis. A dedicated nuclear purge mechanism enables instant, irreversible wiping of local indexed database storage with zero lingering metadata.

05

Stateless Enterprise AI Inference Covenants

Upstream inference calls through Google Gemini and Groq LPU utilize stateless enterprise developer gateways bound by explicit covenants: prompt tokens and image payloads are neither retained nor utilized for foundational model retraining.
